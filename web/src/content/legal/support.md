# Support

Effective date: 2026-09-21

For any question about your Meetli account, appointments, or connected integrations (including Zoom and Google Calendar), contact our team directly at `support@meetli.cc`.

## Connecting and disconnecting Zoom

- Go to Settings → Integrations, choose Connect for Zoom, and authorize access with your Zoom account. Meetli will then create Zoom meeting links for appointments where Zoom is selected as the meeting provider.
- To remove access, go to Settings → Integrations and choose Disconnect for Zoom. This clears the stored Zoom connection from your Meetli account immediately.

## Response time

We aim to respond to support requests promptly. For account security concerns (for example, suspected unauthorized access), please mark your message as urgent.
